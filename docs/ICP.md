# The Vantage — ICP & Positioning Brief

_Synthesized from research: NAR 2024 member profile, Relitix agent attrition data, BoxBrownie / Reel-E / AutoReel pricing, TikTok hook benchmarks, SaaS conversion benchmarks. May 24, 2026._

---

## ONE-SENTENCE ICP

**Newer-to-mid-tier listing agents (0–5 years, $50K–$150K GCI) who need to look like a top producer the night they list — so the seller's neighbors see the Reel and call them next.**

## TOP 3 PERSONAS — ranked by purchase likelihood

### 1. Newer agents (0–3 years, "hungry but broke")
- Age 30–45, median income $8K–$50K GCI, 75% wash out year one.
- Marketing budget <$5K/yr. Every listing is existential.
- Heavy TikTok / Reels user; first-touch reachable on TikTok ads and FB Realtor groups.
- **$39 STARTER is a no-brainer; repeat purchase every new listing.**
- Buying trigger: just signed their 2nd or 3rd listing of the year.

### 2. Mid-tier producing agents (3–15 years, top half)
- Median income $58K, biz spend $8K–$10K/yr, top half spends $30K+.
- 52% of marketing spend is digital; agents allocating 15%+ to video win 49% more listing appointments.
- **Highest LTV — buys $79 PRO and $149.99 STUDIO. Closes 2–4 listings/month.**
- Buying trigger: brokerage / team leader pressure to post more Reels; a competitor's Reel went viral in their farm area.

### 3. Real estate photographers reselling video
- Already charge $250–$1,000+ per listing video; The Vantage = 90%+ margin reseller play.
- Bulk credit consumption, monthly recurring revenue for us.
- Buying trigger: client asked for video, they don't shoot video, they need a fast solution.

**Skip for V1**: brokerages (slow sales cycle), Airbnb hosts (single property, low urgency), contractors (different positioning).

## 5 BUYING TRIGGERS — when the credit card comes out

1. **New listing just signed** — needs content TONIGHT, not in 48 hours (BoxBrownie's turnaround).

## POSITIONING GAP

The Vantage is **the only tool offering truly unlimited cinematic AI listing reels under $150/mo**. Every direct video competitor either caps listings (Reel-E, AutoReel) or charges per-shoot (HomeJab, videographers), and every "unlimited" tool is photo-only.

## ANCHOR PRICING — what $79 PRO beats

- ONE BoxBrownie staged room: $24 × 3 rooms = **$72** (just for stills, no video)
- ONE HomeJab video shoot: **$179 minimum**
- ONE typical videographer shoot: **$300–$1,000**
- Reel-E's 10-listing plan: **$97/mo with hard cap**

**Marketing line:** *"$79 = one freelancer video. The Vantage = unlimited, all month."*

## HOOK PATTERNS THAT CONVERT (TikTok/Meta/Reddit ad-tested)

1. **POV reveal**: "POV: you just listed a $750K home and the photos look like a Zillow nightmare." POV gets 28% higher engagement.
2. **Quiet-insider**: "The AI tool top producers are using but won't tell you about."

- **60 free credits, no card required** → discovery layer (opt-in trial averages 8.9% conversion; opt-out kills 60-70% of signups).
- **Watermark removed on PRO+** → feature gate that lifts freemium conversion from 2.6% → 5.1%.
- **30-day money-back on PRO and STUDIO** → guarantees lift revenue ~6.5% net of refunds.
- Anchor $79 as **MOST CHOSEN**, $149.99 as **BEST VALUE / TEAMS**, $39 as **STARTER**.
