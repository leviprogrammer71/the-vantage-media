## 3. The "Made with Claude" series

**Concept.** A short-form series where a real person hands Claude one listing link and Claude builds a full launch package **live** — you screen-record the chat and the finished reels. It's a product demo, social proof, and category education in one asset, and it's structurally different from every competitor because you're filming a *conversation that does work*, not a UI tour.

**Format (repeatable):**
- 30–60s, vertical, screen-record of the Claude chat + the finished reels cut in.
- **Hook in the first 2 seconds** (the transformation or the claim).
- Show the paste → Claude's plan → the assets appearing → the finished reel.
- End card: "Made with Claude · The Vantage — the first agentic listing tool. thevantage.media."
- Caption always includes the category line + a soft CTA.

**Cadence.** 2–3/week for 6 weeks to establish the category, then 1–2/week. Repurpose each into: a YouTube Short, an IG Reel, a TikTok, an X video, and a still carousel of the before/after.
