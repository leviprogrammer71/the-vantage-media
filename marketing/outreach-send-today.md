# Send-Today Outreach — Format Variants + Google Business Targeting

Same core offer everywhere (**free reel / 60 credits / thevantage.media**). What changes is the **format**, so you can A/B which structure gets replies. Personalize every `[bracket]`. Send each recipient ONE variant; track which format wins.

---

## ⚠️ Cost rule — don't gift reels to cold prospects

Every reel you generate costs ~$2.70. Making a bespoke reel for each cold email doesn't scale for a solo founder. So:

- **COLD outreach → NO reel spend.** Point them to the **/examples showcase** (thevantage.media/examples) and your **one reusable proof reel** (the Fresno clip). Let them make their own with the **free 60 credits** — that's self-serve, capped, and self-selecting. You spend nothing to send.
- **WARM/high-value only → spend ONE reel to close.** If a strong agent replies, or a photographer who could resell, *then* make a reel from their real listing. $2.70 to close a warm lead is worth it; on cold strangers it isn't.

Links to reuse forever (zero marginal cost): `thevantage.media/examples` and your best example/Fresno reel URL.

**Testing note:** send A/B/C to one third each this week; scale the format with the best reply rate. Keep the *offer* identical so you're testing format, not message.

## WARM close (spend ONE reel — only after they engage)

### Format F — The bespoke closer
> **Subject:** made this from your [street] listing
>
> [First name] — you were curious, so I made a reel from your [address] photos: [link]. It's yours to post. If you want to run more, your account already has free credits at thevantage.media.

Use F **only** for a warm reply or a genuinely high-value target (top producer, photographer, small brokerage). One reel, one warm lead — good money. Never send F cold.

## Who to send to — qualify with Google Business

Google Business Profile (Google Maps) is a goldmine because it self-selects for people who **care about being found and marketed** — exactly your buyer.

### Where to pull names
Search Google Maps in target markets:
- `real estate agent [city]`
- `real estate photographer [city]` ← highest leverage (one photographer = many agents)
- `real estate brokerage [city]`
- `property stager [city]` / `home stager [city]`

Work mid-size, marketing-forward metros first (Austin, Phoenix, Nashville, Tampa, San Diego, Charlotte, Denver, Sacramento) — active markets, agents who post.

### Qualification scorecard (send only if 3+ green flags)
**Green flags (send):**
- ✅ Has a **website** linked on their profile (they invest in marketing).
- ✅ **Active** — recent reviews, replies to reviews, or Google Posts in the last ~60 days.
- ✅ Profile photos are **stills only** (no video) — clear opportunity for you.
- ✅ **Solo agent or small team** (2–10) — big enough to spend, small enough to have no in-house video crew.
- ✅ A **findable business email** (on their site's contact/about page).
- ✅ 4.0★+ with a real review count — legit, established operator.

**Red flags (skip):**
- ❌ No website / no reachable email.
- ❌ **Mega-team or franchise with in-house media** (they won't switch).
- ❌ Inactive (no reviews/posts in a year).
- ❌ iBuyer / discount-brokerage / lead-gen mill (not your ICP).
- ❌ Already posting lots of pro video (harder sell; lower priority).

### How to actually collect (do it right)
- Get the **email from their website** (contact/about page), *not* by scraping Maps — Google's ToS prohibits automated Maps scraping, and harvested lists tank deliverability and can violate CAN-SPAM.
- Build a simple sheet: `Name | Business | City | Website | Email | Category (agent/photographer/host) | Score | Format sent | Replied?`
- 20–30 well-qualified, personalized sends beat 500 blasted ones — every time.

### Compliance + deliverability (protect your domain)
- **CAN-SPAM (US B2B cold email is legal)** if you: use a truthful subject, identify yourself, include a **physical mailing address** in the footer, and honor **opt-out** (a simple "reply STOP / not interested and I'll remove you" works for low volume).
- **Outside the US is stricter** — Canada (CASL) and EU (GDPR) generally require opt-in; hold off on cold email there.
- **Warm up + go slow:** ideally send from a **separate domain** (e.g., `try-thevantage.com`) so a spam hit never harms thevantage.media; set up **SPF, DKIM, DMARC**; keep it **under ~30–40/day per inbox** at first; personalize every one.
- **First line must be about THEM** (their listing/city), never a template opener — it's the single biggest reply-rate and deliverability lever.

### Daily target (per person)
25 qualified sends · 3-touch follow-up · log every reply · scale the winning format.
