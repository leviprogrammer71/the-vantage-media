# The Vantage — Zillow Prospecting Kit

The best search list + the best emails, in one system. Backed by 2026 cold-email
benchmark data: plain text gets ~2× the replies of designed HTML on cold sends;
average B2B reply rate is 3–5%, top campaigns 10%+ via targeting + follow-up.
Strategy: **plain text opens, the designed promo closes.**

---

## PART 1 — Build the best search list (Zillow)

### The search recipe

1. Go to `zillow.com/professionals/real-estate-agent-reviews/[city]/`
2. **Specialty filter: "Listing agent" / "Seller's agent"** — NOT property
   management (property managers don't buy listing reels; sellers' agents do).
3. Work these metros first (active, marketing-forward, listing-heavy):
   Phoenix · Austin · Tampa · Nashville · Charlotte · San Diego · Sacramento ·
   Denver · Dallas · Atlanta. Then your home turf (Fresno/Central CA — local
   angle converts above benchmark).

### The scorecard — add to the list only if 4+ boxes tick

| Signal (on the card) | Target | Skip |
|---|---|---|
| Sales last 12 months | **15–80** | 100+ (has in-house media) or <5 (unless "new agent" play) |
| Team size | Solo or 2–5 team | Mega-team / franchise flagship |
| Price range top end | **$500K+** | Sub-$300K only |
| "Top Agent" badge | ✅ pays Zillow = proven tool-buyer | — |
| Reviews | 20–300 at 4.8★+ | Inactive (no recent reviews) |

### The two-click deep check (do this before every send)

Open their profile → open 2–3 of their **active listings**:
- **Media check:** stills only, no video/3D tour → *goldmine; this becomes your
  first line.*
- **Profile keywords that upgrade a lead:** "listing specialist", "seller
  representation", "luxury", "marketing", "new construction", "staging",
  "relocation". Any two of these = priority tier.
- Grab: name, brokerage, one active listing address + price, sales count.
- **Get the email from their brokerage/personal website** (never scrape
  Zillow — ToS + deliverability).

### Tracking sheet columns

`Name | Brokerage | City | Sales/12mo | Price range | Top Agent? | Listing
checked (addr) | Has video? | Email | Source URL | Tier (A/B) | Touch 1 date |
Variant sent | Reply? | Notes`

Daily target: 20–30 **A-tier, fully personalized** sends. Never batch-blast
the raw list.

## PART 2 — The best emails (the 3-touch system)

### TOUCH 1 — plain text, no images, ONE link. (This is the door-opener.)

Pick the variant that matches what you found. First line MUST use their real
data. Subject lines stay lowercase-casual — they read as human.

**Variant A — "the naked listing" (use when their listing has no video — highest converter)**
> **Subject:** your listing on [street]
>
> Hi [First name] — was looking at [address] ([$price]) and noticed it's all
> stills, no video. That listing would make a great cinematic reel — here's
> what one looks like made purely from listing photos:
> thevantage.media/examples#18736-topanga-beach-rd
>
> First one's free if you want to try it on [street]. Worth a look?
>
> Levi · The Vantage

**Variant B — "the volume compliment" (use their sales count)**
> **Subject:** [X] closings last year — quick idea
>
> Hi [First name] — saw you closed [X] homes in [city] last year. At that
> volume, a videographer for every listing doesn't pencil — so most of your
> listings hit Instagram as stills, right?
>
> We turn listing photos into cinematic reels in ~3 minutes. Real example:
> thevantage.media/examples
>
> Free to try on your next one. Want the link?
>
> Levi · The Vantage

**Variant C — "the luxury angle" (use for $700K+ price ranges / "luxury" keyword)**
> **Subject:** the [neighborhood] listing deserved a film
>
> [First name] — your price range says luxury; luxury buyers expect motion.
> We turn the listing photos you already have into cinematic reels — staged
> rooms, golden-hour exteriors, the works. One listing becomes a week of
> content: thevantage.media/examples
>
> First film's on us. Interested?
>
> Levi · The Vantage

Rules: under 90 words · one link · no attachments · no images · send 20–30/day
per inbox · personalize line 1 every time.

### TOUCH 2 — day 3, no reply → NOW send the designed promo

Forward-style, with the branded HTML (vantage-promo-email.html):
> **Subject:** re: [previous subject]
>
> [First name] — easier to show than tell. This is what one listing's photos
> turn into 👇 *(promo email below: proof strip → GIF → 60-free-credits offer)*

The promo earns its keep here: they've seen your name once, so Promotions-tab
risk matters less, and the visual proof does the convincing.

### TOUCH 3 — day 7, breakup (plain text)

> **Subject:** closing your file
>
> No worries [First name] — I'll stop here. The free link stays live whenever
> a listing deserves more than stills: thevantage.media. Rooting for your
> [city] listings either way.

**Reply handling:** any positive reply → offer to make a reel from THEIR
active listing (this is the warm close — one reel, ~$2.70, worth it), or send
the signup link if they're self-serve types.

## PART 3 — Measure like it's an experiment

- Split Touch-1 sends across A/B/C variants evenly for the first 150 sends.
- Success metric = **replies** (not opens/clicks). Benchmark: 3–5% is normal,
  8%+ means the variant wins — kill the others.
- If replies < 2% after 100 sends: the list is the problem, not the copy —
  tighten the scorecard (usually: price range too low or wrong specialty).
- Log every reply verbatim — objections become your next FAQ + video scripts.
